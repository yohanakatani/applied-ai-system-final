# Listening while working out

**Keywords:** workout, exercise, gym, running, energetic, intense, high energy, motivation, tempo

Tempo entrainment is the mechanism that matters: people unconsciously match
movement cadence to musical tempo. That makes BPM the single most useful feature
for exercise playlists, more than genre or mood.

Rough targets: 120–140 BPM for steady cardio, 140–160 for running, 100–120 for
lifting where the rest intervals matter more than continuous pace.

Energy above 0.75 is the usual floor. Valence matters less than expected —
aggressive low-valence tracks perform as well as triumphant high-valence ones
for perceived exertion, so "dark" and "uplifting" are both viable.

**Common mistake:** building a playlist entirely from peak-intensity tracks.
Without contrast the peaks stop registering as peaks.
