# Rock

**Keywords:** rock, intense, guitar, loud, driving, aggressive, anthemic, energetic

Rock is guitar-forward and dynamics-driven. The genre's defining move is
contrast — a restrained verse setting up a loud chorus — so energy readings
often understate how a track actually feels in sequence.

Typical energy runs 0.65 to 0.95, tempo 110 to 160 BPM. Valence varies more
widely than in most genres: rock accommodates both triumphant and bleak moods
at identical energy levels, which is why energy alone is a poor predictor of
whether a rock track feels uplifting or heavy.

**Best for:** workouts, driving, focus through momentum rather than calm.

**Adjacent genres:** metal (heavier, lower valence), punk (faster, rawer),
indie rock (lower production gloss).
