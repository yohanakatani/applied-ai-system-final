# Jazz

**Keywords:** jazz, relaxed, smooth, improvisation, acoustic, sophisticated, evening, warm

Jazz is improvisation over harmonic structure. Recordings capture performances
rather than constructions, which means live-room ambience and dynamic variation
are features rather than production flaws.

Typical energy spans 0.30 to 0.70 depending heavily on subgenre — a ballad and
a hard bop track share a label and almost nothing else. Acousticness runs high.
Tempo is unreliable as a descriptor because rhythmic feel matters more than
literal BPM.

**Best for:** dinner, conversation, evening reading, unhurried work.

**Adjacent genres:** lofi (borrows jazz harmony), soul, r&b.
