# Listening to relax or sleep

**Keywords:** relax, sleep, calm, peaceful, unwind, chill, quiet, evening, rest, acoustic

Music intended for rest works through what it withholds. Sudden dynamic changes
trigger orienting responses even during light sleep, so consistency matters more
than beauty.

What works: energy below 0.30, tempo under 80 BPM, high acousticness or soft
synthesis, minimal percussion, narrow dynamic range. Slow tempo appears to pull
resting heart rate downward over ten to fifteen minutes.

What does not: anything with a build, tracks that end abruptly, high-valence
brightness — cheerfulness is activating, not calming.

**Worth noting:** low valence is not a problem here. Melancholy and calm sit
close together, and the most effective rest music often reads as slightly sad
rather than happy.
