# Metal

**Keywords:** metal, angry, aggressive, heavy, intense, distortion, dark, loud

Metal pushes distortion, tempo, and rhythmic precision to their limits. Energy
readings sit near the ceiling for nearly every track, which makes energy almost
useless for distinguishing within the genre — valence and tempo carry the
signal instead.

Typical energy exceeds 0.85. Valence is characteristically low, often below 0.30,
and that combination — very high energy with very low valence — is close to
unique among genres.

**Best for:** heavy lifting, catharsis, high-intensity work.

**Adjacent genres:** rock (less extreme), hardcore, industrial.
