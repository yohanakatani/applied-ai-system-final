# Ambient

**Keywords:** ambient, chill, calm, atmospheric, spacious, sleep, meditative, texture, peaceful

Ambient prioritizes texture and space over rhythm and melody. Many tracks have
no perceptible beat at all, which makes tempo readings meaningless and energy
readings cluster at the low end.

Typical energy sits below 0.35. Instrumentalness is near-total. Tracks are often
long, since the genre works through duration rather than through structure.

**Best for:** sleep, meditation, deep focus, anxiety reduction. The intended
listening mode is inattention — ambient succeeds when you stop noticing it.

**Adjacent genres:** lofi (more rhythmic), classical minimalism, drone.
