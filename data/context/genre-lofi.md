# Lofi

**Keywords:** lofi, chill, study, focus, ambient, mellow, instrumental, background, calm

Lofi is built around deliberate imperfection — tape hiss, vinyl crackle, slightly
detuned keys, drums that sit a fraction behind the beat. That looseness is the
point. The genre avoids sharp transients and wide dynamic range, which is why it
recedes into the background instead of demanding attention.

Typical energy sits between 0.25 and 0.45, tempo between 70 and 90 BPM. Most
tracks are instrumental or use vocals as texture rather than as a focal point.

**Best for:** studying, reading, low-stakes work, winding down. Listeners
usually want a long uninterrupted stretch rather than standout individual
tracks.

**Adjacent genres:** ambient (less rhythmic), jazz (shared harmonic language,
more active), downtempo.
