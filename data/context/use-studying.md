# Listening while studying

**Keywords:** study, focus, work, concentration, reading, chill, calm, instrumental, background

Research on background music and concentration converges on one finding: lyrics
compete with verbal reasoning. Instrumental tracks are consistently less
disruptive for reading and writing than vocal tracks at the same energy level.

What works: steady tempo, low dynamic range, minimal vocals, energy between 0.25
and 0.45. Predictability is a feature — surprise pulls attention.

What does not: tempo changes, sudden loud sections, familiar songs with
memorized lyrics, anything the listener actively likes enough to notice.

**A useful heuristic:** if you can recall a specific moment from the last twenty
minutes of listening, the music was too interesting for the task.
