# Pop

**Keywords:** pop, happy, upbeat, bright, catchy, euphoric, mainstream, dance

Pop is engineered for immediacy. Hooks arrive early, arrangements are dense and
loudness-maximized, and structure is predictable by design so that a first
listen feels like a second one.

Typical energy runs 0.65 to 0.90 with high valence, though "sad pop" pairs bright
production with low-valence lyrics — a mismatch that audio features cannot
detect, since the instrumental brightness is what gets measured.

**Best for:** social settings, chores, commuting, anywhere requiring low
listening effort.

**Adjacent genres:** indie pop (sparser), edm (more rhythm-led), r&b.
